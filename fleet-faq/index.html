Fleet FAQs

Questions,
Answered

Everything fleet managers ask us before signing a recurring contract. Don’t see your question? Call or text (801) 564-9942.

Do you offer recurring fleet detailing contracts?

Yes — that’s our core business. We set up a recurring schedule (weekly, biweekly, or monthly) for your whole fleet at your yard, with volume pricing. We currently run a 25-dump-truck monthly contract; RigRevive was built around repeat fleet work, not one-off details.

Do you come to our location, or do we bring vehicles to you?

We come to you. RigRevive is fully mobile — we detail at your yard, job site, or facility anywhere from Ogden to Provo. No downtime driving vehicles across town; your fleet stays where it works.

Can you work after hours or overnight?

Yes. Most fleet work happens early mornings, evenings, or overnight so vehicles are ready for the next shift. We schedule around your operations, not the other way around.

How many vehicles can you handle per month?

We currently run a recurring 25-dump-truck monthly contract and have capacity for more. For larger fleets, we scale the schedule — tell us your vehicle count and locations, and we’ll build a plan that covers all of it.

Are you insured? Can you provide proof of insurance?

Yes. RigRevive is a registered LLC and fully insured, and proof of insurance is available on request — which we know most companies and municipalities require before signing anything.

What types of fleet vehicles do you detail?

Dump trucks, semi tractors, box trucks, work trucks, cargo and passenger vans, service vehicles, and municipal fleets. Our specialty is interiors — cabs, sleeper cabs, crew areas — plus sanitation. If it has a cab, we can detail it.

What’s included in a fleet interior detail?

A full cab deep-clean: vacuum, wipe-down of all surfaces, windows, floor mats, stain treatment, odor treatment, and sanitation of high-touch areas (steering wheel, handles, controls). Packages scale from maintenance cleans to full restorations depending on vehicle condition and service frequency.

How does fleet pricing work?

Volume pricing based on vehicle count, vehicle type, condition, and service frequency — recurring contracts cost less per vehicle than one-offs. Request a quote and we’ll price your actual fleet instead of handing you a generic price list that fits nobody.

What areas do you serve?

Utah’s Wasatch Front from Ogden to Provo, including Layton, Salt Lake City, West Valley City, Sandy, Draper, Lehi, and Orem. If your yard is in the corridor, we’re there.

What about water runoff and environmental compliance?

Our fleet work is interior-focused, so there’s minimal water use and no wash runoff to manage. For any exterior add-ons, we follow local wastewater rules. One less compliance headache for your team.

How do we get started?

Request a quote or call/text (801) 564-9942. We’ll talk through your fleet size, locations, and schedule, then propose a recurring plan with straightforward pricing. Most fleets are up and running within a week or two.
