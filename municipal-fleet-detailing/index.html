Fleet FAQs

Questions,
Answered

Straight answers before you sign a recurring contract. Don’t see your question? Call or text (801) 564-9942.

Are you insured? Can you provide proof of insurance?

Yes. RigRevive is a registered LLC and fully insured, and proof of insurance is available on request — which we know most cities, counties, and agencies require before signing anything.

Which municipal vehicles do you detail?

School buses, city and county vehicles, public works trucks, and service vehicles. Our specialty is interiors — cabs, crew areas, and passenger areas — plus sanitation of high-touch surfaces.

Do you sanitize shared-use vehicles?

Yes. Sanitation of high-touch areas — steering wheels, handles, controls — is standard in every fleet interior detail. Quarterly interior sanitation programs are available for vehicles with rotating drivers or passengers.

What about water runoff and environmental compliance?

Our fleet work is interior-focused, so there’s minimal water use and no wash runoff to manage — one less compliance headache for your team. For any exterior add-ons, we follow local wastewater rules.

Can you work around our operations and shifts?

Yes. Most fleet work happens early mornings, evenings, or overnight so vehicles are ready for the next shift. We schedule around your operations, not the other way around.
